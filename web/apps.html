Apps

Programs that run on STELLAR - every one of them has run on a real Elf2K with its 1976 RCA 1802 at 3 MHz. The pictures are the apps' own screens, captured from the same firmware running in the simulator. Click an app to load it into the Simulator and run it full size; click the terminal and play.

Several at once

STELLAR runs up to four programs side by side on the one 1802. The Multitasking apps above open on the Multitasking page, where you can watch the scheduler hand the chip from VM to VM; the home page runs four apps at once.

Every app is also a download for a real Elf2K: see Run it on a real Elf2K. Some are written in Stellar C.
