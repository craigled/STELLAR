Serial terminal

Click the terminal and type to send keys to the running program — Enter, Backspace and Ctrl-key combinations all go through. On a phone or tablet, tap the terminal for the keyboard; the arrow keys, Esc and Enter are in the row underneath. The input box above just pre-loads keystrokes before you start.
